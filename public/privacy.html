Privacy Policy

Effective [date]

This policy explains what [your name or company] ("we") collects when you use Openline, and why.

1. What we don't collect

We never receive, record or store your video, audio or chat messages. They travel directly between your browser and the other person's, encrypted. When a direct connection isn't possible, they pass through a relay server still encrypted, and the relay can't read them.

2. What we store

  • Account: your email address, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, when you signed up, and when you passed the age check. We don't keep your date of birth.
  • Login sessions: a hashed session identifier and when it expires.
  • Feedback you send us: your message, when you sent it, and your browser type, linked to your account.
  • Safety records: reports you make or receive (who, when, and the reason chosen), pairs of people who won't be matched again, and whether an account is suspended.

3. What we use briefly and don't keep

  • IP address: used to connect you and to limit login, sign-up and skip attempts. Kept only in memory, for a few hours at most. Our hosting provider may keep it in server logs for a short time.
  • Interests you type: sent to us only while you're looking for a match. Your browser remembers them on your device.

4. The other person sees your IP address

Because calls connect browsers directly, the person you're talking to can technically see your IP address, which can show your approximate location. This is how peer-to-peer calls work.

5. Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. It's required for the site to work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Other services involved

  • Hosting: [hosting provider, e.g. Render] runs our servers and stores the data above.
  • Connection servers: Google's STUN server helps your browser find its network address. [TURN provider] relays encrypted calls when a direct connection fails.
  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

7. Why we use it

To run your account and match you for calls, to keep Openline safe (age check, reports, bans), and to meet legal obligations. We may share information with law enforcement when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm, including reporting child sexual exploitation.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Login sessions: up to 30 days, or until you log out.
  • Safety records (reports, blocks, suspensions): [period, e.g. 1 year], including after account deletion, so banned people can't simply come back.

9. Your choices and rights

You can change your password, log out everywhere, or delete your account in Account settings. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use them.

10. Children

Openline is not for anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

11. Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date.
